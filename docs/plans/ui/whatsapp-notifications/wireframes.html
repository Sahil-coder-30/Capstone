WhatsApp and notifications screens

Screens for kafka-whatsapp-architecture.md. K1 to K4 have no screens: they move mail and usage onto Kafka without changing what users see. K5 adds the "link phone" screen and WhatsApp control of runs. K6 adds the admin portal's notify module (announcements, delivery log, failed deliveries). Spec: screens.md.
